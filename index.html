3. The Heuristic Search Hypothesis

The Heuristic Search Hypothesis: The solutions to problems are represented as symbol structures. A physical symbol system exercises its intelligence in problem solving by search—that is, by generating and progressively modifying symbol structures until a solution structure is found.
